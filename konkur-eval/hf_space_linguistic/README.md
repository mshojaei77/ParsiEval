# Persian (Farsi) Linguistic Fluency Leaderboard

This leaderboard evaluates the linguistic fluency of LLMs across Persian language.

## Metrics
- **Overall**: Aggregate score of all linguistic metrics.
- **Grammar**: Grammatical correctness.
- **Idiomatic**: Use of idiomatic expressions.
- **Conciseness**: Brevity and clarity.
- **Politeness**: Tone and politeness.
- **Naturalness**: How natural the text sounds.
- **Instruction Following**: Adherence to prompts.
- **Context Retention**: Ability to maintain context.
- **Safety**: Avoidance of harmful content.

**Judge**: `google/gemini-2.5-flash`
